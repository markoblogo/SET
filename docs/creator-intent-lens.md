# Creator Intent Lens

`SET` can export a compact creator-intent lens for creative, editorial, and
brand-forward work where literal compliance can miss the point.

## Use when

- the brief is emotionally clear but operationally incomplete;
- a page, artifact, or proposal risks becoming technically correct and
  humanly wrong;
- the task depends on voice, feel, first impression, or authorship;
- the agent is polishing structure while the intended outcome stays blurry.

## Core checks

Before widening into implementation or polish, state:

1. what the person is actually trying to make possible;
2. what should remain recognizably theirs;
3. which decision most changes first-use experience;
4. whether one sharp clarifying question is needed.

If ambiguity does not block a real decision, move. Do not turn understanding
into a survey.

## Review line

When the work is user-facing, do not stop at demo quality. Check the result
across:

- first use;
- failure;
- recovery;
- repetition.

If the work technically functions but drains confidence, ownership, or desire
to continue, the lens is not satisfied.

## Boundary

This is a disabled interpretation aid, not a scope-expansion license. It does
not override explicit instructions, factual constraints, safety boundaries, or
exact output requests. Do not invent hidden requirements in its name.
